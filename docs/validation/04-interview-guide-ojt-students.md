# Validation 4/5 — Interview Guide: OJT Students

> Part of [validation](../validation.md) · ← [Previous](03-interview-guide-dost-scholars.md) · [Next](05-coordinator-checklist-review.md) →

## ✅ What to do with this file

- [ ] Use this guide for segments **C** (about to start OJT) and **D** (finished OJT in the last 12 months).
- [ ] Ask about **past events**, not opinions.
- [ ] Do **not** describe our product until the last section.
- [ ] Note every document they mention. Compare with the draft checklist in [Part 5](05-coordinator-checklist-review.md).

---

## 1. Warm-up (3 min)
1. What school, course, and year? Is your school public or private?
2. When is/was your OJT? How many hours are required?

## 2. Finding a host company (5 min)
3. Walk me through how you found (or are finding) your OJT company.
4. Where did you look? Who helped? How long did it take?
5. Did your school require the company to have a MOA or meet certain standards? What happened if it didn't?

## 3. Requirements (10 min)
6. Tell me about the requirements you had to complete before you could start. List them as best you remember.
7. Which one took the longest or was the hardest? Why?
8. Did anything get rejected, delayed, or lost? What happened next?
9. How did you know what was required and by when (memo, group chat, coordinator, seniors)?
10. How much did the requirements cost you (medical exam, notarization, printing, travel)? *(Rough idea only.)*
11. Where did you keep track of them? *(Ask to see the checklist/folder/chat if comfortable. Don't keep a copy.)*

## 4. During and after OJT (5 min) — mainly segment D
12. How did you log your hours and submit reports/journals?
13. What did you have to submit at the end to get credit?
14. Was anything about your schedule or academic load a problem while on OJT?

## 5. Other opportunities (2 min)
15. Besides OJT, how do you find internships, scholarships, or competitions? Ever seen a scam post?

## 6. Concept check & commitment (4 min) — only now describe the idea

> "We're exploring a free tool with a checklist of OJT requirements based on CHED rules plus your school's own items, with deadlines and reminders, and a summary you can show your coordinator."

16. What would make that actually useful for you? What would make you not trust it?
17. **Commitment asks** (record yes/no):
    - Can we contact you to try an early version?
    - Can you share your school's actual requirements list (the memo/handout, no personal info)?
    - Can you introduce us to your OJT coordinator or 2 classmates?
