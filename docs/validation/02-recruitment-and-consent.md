# Validation 2/6 — Recruitment, Consent & Interview Logistics

> Part of [validation](../validation.md) · ← [Previous](01-idea-validation-framework.md) · [Next](03-interview-guide-dost-scholars.md) →

## ✅ What to do with this file

- [ ] Recruit across **several schools, public and private** (we target all of them).
- [ ] Send the recruitment messages (§2) through orgs, friends, and public groups.
- [ ] Read the consent script (§3) at the start of every interview.
- [ ] Log every interview in [`templates/interview-log.csv`](templates/interview-log.csv) using codes (P01, P02…), not names.

---

## 1. Who to Interview

| Segment | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Current DOST scholars** | 8–10 | Requirements, stipend delays, understanding of ROS before graduation |
| **B. DOST graduates serving ROS** (1–4 years after graduation) | 8–10 | **They feel ROS tracking pain the most.** Current scholars haven't started serving yet |
| **C. Students about to start OJT** | 5–8 | Current requirement process |
| **D. Students who finished OJT in the last 12 months** | 5–8 | Can describe the whole process from memory |
| **E. OJT coordinators** | 3–5, from different schools | Checklist review (see [Part 5](05-coordinator-checklist-review.md)) |
| **F. 3rd/4th-year IT/CS/IS students** | **15+** (sprint) | **Current priority.** How they find hackathons, CTFs, OJT, and tech scholarships, and what they missed ([sprint §5](06-30-day-validation-sprint.md#5-interview-guide-itcs-students-segment-f-20-minutes)) |

During the [30-day sprint](06-30-day-validation-sprint.md), only segment F is required. Start with about 8 per segment. If answers keep repeating, you've learned enough. If they disagree, go up to 15–20.

**Mix:** at least 3 schools, both public (SUC/LUC) and private, and more than one region where possible.

## 2. Recruitment Messages

### For campus IT org officers (Taglish)

> Hi [Name]! Kami ay team ng students na gumawa ng **Oportune**, isang libreng site na naglilista ng **hackathons, CTFs, OJT, at tech scholarships** para sa college students, at bawat isa ay chine-check ng tao sa mismong source, may reminder bago ang deadline.
>
> Bago kami mag-offer ng kahit ano, gusto muna naming malaman: **ano ang kailangan ng org n'yo ngayong sem?** (content para sa page, attendees sa events, member perks?)
>
> Ang kaya naming ibigay, libre lahat: isang ready-to-post na **weekly "opportunity drop"**, libreng listing ng events n'yo, at talk tungkol sa scams o "paano namin ginawa." Ang hihilingin lang namin: isang post ng link (may sariling tag ang org n'yo para makita namin kung ilan ang dumating), at 5–10 members na pwedeng ma-interview nang 20 minutes.
>
> Walang bayad, walang member data na kukunin. Pwede ba tayong mag-usap this week?

### For IT/CS students (segment F, Taglish)

> Hi! Kami ay grupo ng IT/CS students na nag-aaral kung paano nakakahanap ang mga estudyante ng **hackathons, CTFs, OJT, at tech scholarships**, at kung bakit minsan nami-miss natin ang deadlines.
>
> Naghahanap kami ng makakausap for **20 minutes** (video call or chat). **Hindi ito sales pitch.** Gusto lang namin marinig ang experience mo. Confidential ang sagot mo, at hindi namin ilalabas ang pangalan mo.
>
> Pwede ka ba this week? 🙏 Kung may kilala kang 3rd/4th-year IT/CS student, pa-share na rin!

### For DOST and OJT segments (A–D, Taglish)

> Hi! Kami ay grupo ng mga estudyante na nag-aaral kung paano hina-handle ng mga students ang **OJT requirements** at **DOST scholarship requirements/Return of Service**.
>
> Naghahanap kami ng makakausap for **20–30 minutes** (video call or chat). **Hindi ito sales pitch.** Gusto lang namin marinig ang experience mo. Confidential ang sagot mo, at hindi namin ilalabas ang pangalan mo.
>
> Pwede ka ba this week? 🙏 Kung may kilala ka ring DOST scholar o kakatapos lang mag-OJT, pa-share na rin!

### For coordinators (formal)

> Good day, [Sir/Ma'am] [Name],
>
> We are a student team studying how Philippine colleges manage Student Internship Program requirements under CHED CMO No. 104, s. 2017. We prepared a draft requirements checklist and would be grateful for 30 minutes of your time to review it and tell us what is missing or incorrect.
>
> We are not selling anything. Your feedback will only be used to improve a free tool for students, and we will not quote you by name without permission.
>
> Would you be available for a short meeting or call in the next two weeks?

### Where to post
- Student org group chats (computer science/IT/engineering orgs, DOST scholar associations)
- Friends at other schools — ask each interviewee for 2 referrals
- Public scholarship and OJT groups (follow group rules; ask admins first)
- School career/OJT offices for coordinators

## 3. Consent Script (read at the start)

> "Thanks for joining. This takes about 25 minutes. We're learning how students deal with OJT and scholarship requirements. There are no right or wrong answers.
>
> We'll take notes. **May we record the audio?** It's only for our notes and will be deleted after we write them up. We won't use your name. We'll call you something like 'P07.'
>
> Please don't share your scholarship contract number, ID numbers, or medical details; we don't need them. You can skip any question or stop anytime. Okay to start?"

## 4. Privacy Rules for the Team

Interviews collect personal data, so the Data Privacy Act applies to you too.

- Use participant codes (P01…) in all notes. Keep the name↔code list in **one** private file that only the lead can access.
- Record only with explicit consent; delete recordings after writing notes.
- Don't collect contract numbers, IDs, medical info, or exact amounts tied to a name.
- If a scholar is worried about a penalty, **don't give advice**. Point them to DOST-SEI or their school's scholarship office.
- Delete the name↔code list when validation ends, unless participants agreed to be contacted for the beta.

## 5. Logistics

- 2 people per interview: one asks, one takes notes.
- Google Meet or Messenger call; in-person is better if possible.
- Thank participants with a small token (load, snack) if budget allows. This is not a payment for positive answers.
- Write notes within 24 hours.
- Suggested timeline: Week 1 recruit + first 10 interviews; Week 2 next 15 + coordinator reviews; Week 3 synthesis and decisions.
