# Validation 3/5 — Interview Guide: DOST Scholars & Graduates

## ✅ What to do with this file

- [ ] Use this guide for segments **A** (current scholars) and **B** (graduates serving ROS).
- [ ] Ask about **past events**, not opinions. Follow up with "Then what happened?"
- [ ] Do **not** describe our product until the last section.
- [ ] Mark each pain as **prompted** or **unprompted** in your notes.

---

## Interviewer reminders

- Let them talk 80% of the time.
- Good follow-ups: "Can you walk me through that?" "What did you do next?" "How did that feel?" "How long did it take?"
- Bad questions (don't ask): "Would you use…?" "Wouldn't it be great if…?" "How much would you pay?"
- If they get stressed about penalties, don't advise. Refer them to DOST-SEI or the scholarship office.

## 1. Warm-up (3 min)
1. What school, course, and year are you in (or when did you graduate)?
2. Which DOST scholarship do you have (e.g., RA 7687, RA 10612, merit)? When did it start?

## 2. Requirements and submissions (7 min)
3. Tell me about the **last time** you had to submit something to DOST or your scholarship coordinator. Walk me through it step by step.
4. How did you find out what to submit and when?
5. Did anything go wrong (missing document, late, rejected)? What happened?
6. Where do you keep track of your scholarship deadlines and documents today? *(Ask to see it if they're comfortable. Don't keep a copy.)*

## 3. Stipends (5 min, optional — skip if uncomfortable)
7. Tell me about the last time your stipend was released. Was it when you expected?
8. If it was late, what did you do in the meantime? *(Don't ask for amounts or lender names.)*
9. How did you find out when it would arrive?

## 4. Return of Service (8 min)

**For current scholars (A):**
10. What do you know about your Return of Service obligation? How did you learn it?
11. Have you looked at your contract terms about service or refunds? What do you remember?
12. Do you know anyone who had a problem with their ROS (delays, working abroad, wrong field)? What happened?

**For graduates serving ROS (B):**
10. Walk me through what you did after graduating to start your Return of Service.
11. How do you know how many months you've served and how many are left?
12. Tell me about the last report or document you had to submit for ROS. What was hard?
13. Has anything made it unclear whether your job counts (field, location, gaps between jobs)?
14. Have you ever tried to calculate what you'd owe if you couldn't finish? How?

## 5. Other opportunities (3 min)
15. How did you find this scholarship originally? How do you find other opportunities (internships, competitions)?
16. Have you ever seen a fake or scam scholarship post? What happened?

## 6. Concept check & commitment (4 min) — only now describe the idea

> "We're exploring a free tool where you can track your scholarship requirements and Return of Service months, with reminders, and estimate penalties based on your contract terms."

17. What's the first thing you'd want to do with that? What would worry you about it?
18. **Commitment asks** (record yes/no):
    - Can we contact you to try an early version?
    - Can you introduce us to 2 other scholars or graduates?
    - Would you be willing to show us how you track it now in a follow-up?
