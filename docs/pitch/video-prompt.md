# 1-Minute Video Prompt (Problem → Solution)

Makes a 60-second MP4 for Oportune in the app's "campus sticker board" look, for Reels/TikTok (9:16) and for pitches (16:9).

## How this works (read first)

AI video tools make **short clips** (about 5–10 seconds each in Veo, Sora, Runway, Kling, Pika), and they **garble on-screen text and app screens**. So the plan is:

1. **Generate 6 short clips** with the scene prompts below (each one repeats the same style block, so they match).
2. **Use a real screen recording of Oportune** for the solution scenes (phone screen recording of Browse → save → reminder). Never let the AI draw the app.
3. **Put it together in CapCut or Canva**: clips + screen recording + voiceover + captions + music → export MP4.

If your tool has one "storyboard" or "extend" mode (Sora storyboard, Veo/Flow scenebuilder), paste the **Master prompt** instead and let it chain the scenes.

Before you start, check: no real brand logos (no Facebook/Messenger/Google logos), no real people's faces copied from photos, no numbers we can't prove. The video has no traction numbers on purpose; any number added later must come from the [30-day validation sprint scorecard](../validation/06-30-day-validation-sprint.md#3-weekly-scorecard).

Wording follows [final decisions §1c](../research/final-decisions.md#1c-updates-after-the-court-review-2026-10-26): the line is "**Never miss a deadline.** Student opportunities in tech, each one checked by a person against its source." Say **"checked"**, never "verified". The pilot leads with hackathons, CTFs, OJT, and tech scholarships.

## Style block (paste at the end of EVERY scene prompt)

```
STYLE: flat 2D motion graphics, "campus sticker board" look. Cream paper background (#FFF6E5) with subtle paper grain. Every object is a flat sticker card with a thick black outline (3 px) and a hard offset black drop shadow (no blur). Bold pop colors only: sunflower yellow #FFD23F, tomato red #FF6B57, sky blue #7CC4FF, mint #6EE7B7, lilac #C4B5FD, lime #C6F26B, ink black #111111. Playful, snappy motion: stickers pop in with a slight bounce and small tilt (−4° to 4°), quick whip transitions, no slow fades. Filipino college students drawn as simple flat illustrated characters (diverse, school uniforms or casual), friendly faces. No real logos, no brand names, no readable text unless specified, no photorealism, no 3D, no gradients, no lens flares. Clean, uncluttered, centered composition with safe margins for captions.
```

## Scene prompts (≈10 s each)

**Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:08) · The scroll**
```
A Filipino college student sits on a jeepney seat at dusk, scrolling a phone. Above the phone, a chaotic tower of flat sticker "posts" flies past fast: scholarship flyers, internship posters, hackathon cards, all overlapping and blurred by speed. The student's eyes widen, overwhelmed. Camera pushes in on the phone. Ends on a sticker card stamped with a tomato-red "CLOSED" stamp that slams down with a bounce. [STYLE BLOCK]
```

**Scene 2 — Problem: scattered (0:08–0:16)**
```
Top-down view of a cluttered cork board covered in dozens of mismatched sticker flyers, pinned group-chat bubbles, and sticky notes. Lines of red string connect them in a tangled mess. A student's hand tries to grab one flyer and three others fall off. Quick shake. [STYLE BLOCK]
```

**Scene 3 — Problem: too late (0:16–0:24)**
```
A big flat sticker calendar with a sunflower-yellow deadline circle. A student character runs toward it in a cartoon sprint, but the calendar page flips forward and the circle turns tomato red. The student stops, slumps, a small rain cloud sticker appears above their head. [STYLE BLOCK]
```

**Scene 4 — Problem: scams (0:24–0:30)**
```
A suspicious sticker flyer with a shiny fake gold seal slides in, waving a peso coin sign and a "pay first" hand gesture (no readable words). The flyer's edges crack, revealing it is hollow; it flips over showing a red warning triangle sticker. A student steps back and shakes their head. [STYLE BLOCK]
```

**Scene 5 — Turn: the clean-up (0:30–0:38)**
```
All the messy flyers from the cork board get sucked into a single phone screen held up by the student, with a satisfying whoosh. The phone glows; a neat stack of tidy, evenly spaced sticker cards slides into a single clean column, each with a small mint check-mark badge. The background turns from chaotic to calm cream. The student smiles. Leave the phone screen area plain cream (it will be replaced with a real screen recording). [STYLE BLOCK]
```

**Scene 6 — Ending: friends sharing (0:52–0:60)**
```
Three Filipino students in a campus hallway each hold up a phone; a sticker card hops from one phone to the next like a passed note, leaving a small trail of yellow sparkles. They high-five. Camera pulls back to a wide shot as sticker confetti in all pop colors bursts and settles, leaving the center of the frame empty and cream for the logo and call to action. [STYLE BLOCK]
```

**Solution footage (0:38–0:52) — real screen recording, not AI**
Record on your phone (9:16), in this order, about 14 seconds:
1. Browse: "Closing this week" strip (2 s)
2. Open "Para sa'yo ba?" and choose IT & Computer Science + 3rd year (3 s)
3. Open a listing: the "Why you can trust this" box with the source link + "Checked by our team on <date>" (pick a dated hackathon/CTF/OJT listing; the Official badge only shows on `.gov.ph` / `.edu.ph` sources) (3 s)
4. Tap Save; show the reminder note (7 days and 1 day before) (3 s)
5. Tap Share → Messenger (3 s)

Put the recording inside a flat phone frame with a thick black outline and offset shadow on a cream background, tilted 3°, so it matches the AI scenes.

## Captions, music, export

- **Captions:** burned in (most people watch muted). Font: Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold (or any very bold rounded sans), black text on a yellow #FFD23F sticker box with a black outline. Max 4 words per caption. Keep them above the bottom 15% (app buttons cover it on Reels/TikTok).
- **Logo ending (0:55–1:00):** the Oportune ticket-stub logo (yellow card, black outline, dashed line, black dot) pops in center + tagline + link or QR. Make it in Canva, not with AI.
- **Music:** upbeat lo-fi or light pop, around 110–120 BPM, royalty-free (CapCut/Canva library, YouTube Audio Library). Duck it under the voice (−18 dB). A "whoosh" on scene 5 and a "pop" on each sticker.
- **Export:** MP4, H.264, 1080×1920 (9:16) for Reels/TikTok/Stories; make a 1920×1080 (16:9) version for pitch slides by placing the vertical video on a cream background with sticker decorations.
- **Length check:** 55–60 s. If longer, cut scene 4 first.

## Before posting

- [ ] No real logos or brand names inside AI clips; no garbled text visible
- [ ] Every claim is true: free, checked by a person against the source (not "verified"; Official badge only for `.gov.ph` / `.edu.ph`), reminders 7 days + 1 day, filters by region/course/year
- [ ] No user, partner, or traction numbers unless they come from the sprint scorecard
- [ ] The link in the ending works on mobile data
- [ ] Captions readable on a small phone at arm's length
- [ ] Music is licensed for the platform you post on
