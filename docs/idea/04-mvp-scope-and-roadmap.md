## 9. MVP Scope (Phase 1)

1. Public opportunity list and detail pages (no login needed)
2. "Closing soon" view, search, category filters
3. Official-site badge, source link, last-checked date
4. Report button for wrong or suspicious listings
5. Share button with link previews for Messenger/FB
6. Google sign-in + quick profile (school, course, year level, interests)
7. Save listings
8. Deadline reminders by email and phone push notification, with on/off controls
9. Fast, phone-first, installable app
10. Admin panel: add, edit, publish, expire listings; import `data/opportunities.csv`
11. Automatic expiry of listings past their deadline
12. Delete account
13. Basic analytics (views, official-link clicks, saves, shares, reminders opened)

## 10. Roadmap

| Phase | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **0 — Curate & post (now)** | Telegram channel or FB page; post 3–5 checked opportunities a day from the spreadsheet; refresh the spreadsheet every 1–2 weeks | Builds the audience and the first data. Track followers and link clicks. |
| **1 — MVP** | Everything in §9 | Launch to the channel's followers |
| **2 — Track & prepare** | Simple application tracker, OJT requirements checklist, "for you" filter, **document uploads**, **AI helper** | Only after the MVP shows students save and come back |
| **3 — Grow** | **Android app** (iPhone after), **social media sourcing** into the curator inbox, more categories and regional listings, Messenger/Telegram reminders, offline viewing of saved items, AI-assisted admin entry, DOST ROS info page | Based on what students use most |
| **Later / maybe** | DOST ROS tracker, organizer submissions, school or employer features, payments/monetization | Only if there's a clear need |

## 11. What We Won't Build

- Payments or subscriptions (not needed now; see §12)
- Loan referrals
- Scraping of personal profiles or private groups, or storing anyone's personal data from social media

## 12. Monetization (Deferred)

**No payments.** Students always use the platform for free.

Possible future revenue, only after the platform has real users: promoted listings for organizers (clearly labeled), employer internship listings, or school partnerships. That would need its own payment, legal, and tax decisions, which aren't part of these docs.
