## 4. Users

### Who
**College students in the Philippines, at any public or private school, any course, any year level.** Not companies, not schools, not graduates (for now).

### What students are like (and what it means for the product)

| Reality | Product decision |
|---|---|
| Phone first, prepaid data, often cheap phones | Light, fast pages; installable to home screen |
| Live in Messenger, FB, and group chats | One-tap share with good link previews; email alone isn't enough for reminders |
| Busy; act when a deadline is near | Deadlines shown first; "closing soon" view; reminders |
| Wary of scams | Source link, last-checked date, official-site badge, report button |
| Little or no money | Everything free, no payments |
| Won't sign up just to look | Browse without an account; sign up only to save |
| Hate long forms | Google sign-in + 4 quick profile questions (school, course, year, interests) |

### Student types

| Type | Main need |
|---|---|
| **Scholarship hunter** | Scholarships and financial aid that fit their course and year |
| **Career builder** | Internships, OJT placements, graduate programs |
| **Tech competitor** | Hackathons, CTFs, programming contests |
| **Self-learner** | Free courses and certifications |
| **OJT student** (Phase 2) | A checklist to track OJT requirements |

## 5. Product Principles

1. **Free for students.** No payments, subscriptions, or paywalls.
2. **Browse first, sign up later.** Listings are public; an account is only needed to save and get reminders.
3. **Trust first.** Every listing shows its source and last-checked date. Official `.gov.ph` / `.edu.ph` sources get a badge.
4. **No fabricated or unchecked listings.** Every listing has a traceable source and is reviewed by a person before publishing.
5. **Deadline first.** The most urgent opportunities are the easiest to see.
6. **Phone first and data-lite.** Fast on cheap phones and slow connections.
7. **Privacy by default.** Collect only school, course, year level, and interests. No IDs, documents, or financial info.
8. **No AI facing students.** Information about eligibility and deadlines comes from official sources, checked by a person.
9. **Simple beats complete.** Ship the smallest useful version, then improve based on what students actually use.
