# Opening: Defense

The case asks whether to keep investing development time, not whether to raise money or scale. On that question, the record supports going ahead.

1. **The product is nearly ready to test, so continuing is cheap.** The audit lists "about 8,300 lines in src/, 21 pages, 8 API routes", reminders by push and email, a curator admin panel, "RLS enabled on every table", and "Database blocks publishing anything flagged needs-check." The obvious objection is that it has zero users. But what stands between it and a real test is setup work ("set up email, captcha and domain", rotate keys), not a rebuild. Stopping now throws away the cheapest route to real evidence.

2. **The team has already guarded against overbuilding and set its own kill rule.** In their words: "The app isn't the problem now. Content, a live link, and users are." and "Don't build more features until steps 1-3 ... are done." The kill rule was written in advance: "pivot if weekly active students < 300 after 12 weeks, or < 20% return in week 4." The fear is that a founder sinks time into a dead idea. A plan with a dated exit limits that risk.

3. **The beachhead niche has a visible gap.** The research found "No dedicated Philippine hackathon directory" and found CTFtime's 2026 list "shows 37 events, none PH-organized." Meanwhile, local events like DICT Hack4Gov run outside that index. Aiming at "3rd/4th-year IT/CS/IS students" during "OJT season" focuses the effort. I concede that general scholarships are crowded: "Scholarships PH ~639K followers".

4. **Its trust features answer the main competitor's weakness.** The desk research calls Facebook groups "the actual main competitor", with "high scam prevalence". Oportune already has an "official-source badge", a "last checked by our team" date, a report form, and the rule that "a person always publishes", plus 7-day and 1-day deadline reminders that a news feed does not give.

**Concessions:** Monetization is unproven ("The PHP 500-1,500 price comes from a desk-research report, not from any organizer"). The interview log is empty. 52 of 79 listings have no fixed date, and 63 of the 79 sources are "other", which weakens the trust claim. The record has no primary demand evidence. So the next development time must go to deploying and to interviews, not to new features.
