# Opening statement: Prosecution

The case asks to "keep investing development time." The record shows development time is not the shortage.

**1. No evidence of demand.** "Users: 1 (the founder)." The interview log is empty: "zero interviews recorded." "No primary survey backs how students find opportunities." The market evidence is "two AI-generated deep-research reports." *Cost:* every hour goes into an unconfirmed problem. The team's own kill rule ("< 300 weekly active students after 12 weeks") cannot start, because nothing is deployed.

**2. The team's own plan says stop building.** It reads: "The app isn't the problem now. Content, a live link, and users are," and "Don't build more features until steps 1-3 are done." Yet on 2026-10-25 it added eligibility filters, calendar export, a submission form and an OJT checklist. Meanwhile "No admin account created yet," and secrets "pasted in chat" are still not rotated. *Cost:* 8,300 lines of code, no URL, and the pattern is repeating.

**3. Facebook is the free incumbent and the source of content.** The research calls Facebook groups "the actual main competitor": Scholarships PH has "~639K followers." Oportune sources from "a public organizer post (mostly on Facebook)." *Cost:* Oportune sits downstream of the channel it hopes to replace, so it is always slower and never the original.

**4. The core promise does not match the content.** The tagline is "Never miss a deadline," but "52 of 79 have no fixed date." Reminders fire only on "saved, dated listings," and recurring tracking is "only planned." Source trust is "63 'other'" out of 79. Freshness depends on "a daily 15-minute routine" done by one person. *Cost:* "verified" fails once that person stops, and stale listings "undermine trust."

**5. No revenue path.** "No organizer... has been asked to pay." The PHP 500-1,500 price "comes from a desk-research report, not from any organizer." Year 1 depends on grants. Similar products elsewhere were sold or shut down: Fastweb was "sold during Monster's 2025 bankruptcy" and Going Merry "closed March 2026." *Cost:* a product that is free forever, with no tested buyer, run by one person.
