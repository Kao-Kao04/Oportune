# Rebuttal: Defense

**1. No evidence of demand. Survives as a fact, fails as a verdict.** One user and zero interviews is the record of a project begun "2026-10-23," three days ago. The demand evidence is missing because nothing has been tested yet, and testing needs the deployed app plus the team's own kill rule.

**2. The plan says stop building. Survives.** The 2026-10-25 features broke the team's own rule. But what fixes this is putting the work in a different order, not abandoning the project. The remaining steps are a database, an admin account, key rotation and a domain. These are hours of work, not a rebuild.

**3. Facebook is the incumbent. Partly survives.** Facebook pages broadcast posts. They do not offer saves, deadline reminders, eligibility filters or "last checked" dates, which Oportune has already built. In the beachhead niche the record finds "no dedicated Philippine hackathon directory" and no PH-organized events among CTFtime's 37 upcoming.

**4. The promise does not match the content. Survives, and it is the strongest point.** "52 of 79" undated listings weaken "never miss a deadline." The re-check queue for rolling/TBA listings exists, but recurring tracking does not. The record is silent on whether one curator can keep up.

**5. No revenue path. Survives but is premature.** No price has been tested. Opportunity Desk shows that "paid promotion" can fund a free reader product. Fastweb and Going Merry were acquired first, which shows buyers existed.

**Decisive point:** Point 2. The question is whether the next unit of time goes to deploying and running the team's own 12-week kill test with two orgs, which is cheap and conclusive, or to more features.
