# Rebuttal: Prosecution

1. **Survives only in part.** Deploying is cheap, but that argues for a test, not for "keep investing development time". The team's own status line says the bottleneck is "Content, a live link, and users", and none of those is code. Four more features were built on 2026-10-25 while there was still no deployment, no admin account and no interviews.

2. **Does not survive.** The kill rule only applies "after 12 weeks" from a January 2027 soft launch, so it decides nothing until about April 2027. It has no criterion for interviews, partner sign-up or anyone paying, and nothing in the record says where 300 weekly users would come from: "No partnerships", no org has agreed to anything.

3. **Weak.** "No dedicated Philippine hackathon directory" is marked [U], and the same research says PH hackathons already appear on Luma, GDG pages, DEVCON and Facebook. CTFtime shows 4 ranked Philippine teams. A gap this small may exist because demand is small, and the record has no student evidence either way.

4. **Does not survive, by the defense's own concession.** 63 of 79 sources are "other", and the badge only covers .gov.ph/.edu.ph. 52 of 79 listings have no fixed date, so the reminders, which work only on "saved, dated listings", will not fire for most of the content. The reminder jobs "have never run". No user has been asked whether they trust it.

**The point to decide on:** the defense concedes that "the next development time must go to deploying and to interviews, not to new features." That is not the case as stated. With zero interviews and zero users, the charge asks whether more development time is justified, and both sides now agree it is not.
